# Accessibility

_Last reviewed 2026-08-26 · contact meleona.support@gmail.com_

Meleona aims to meet **WCAG 2.2 level AA**. This page says what has been done,
what is measured, and — the part most statements leave out — **where it still
falls short**, so nobody has to discover a gap the hard way.

## What has been done

- **Contrast is measured, not eyeballed.** Every text colour in the stylesheet
  was computed against the WCAG formula on the ground it actually sits on -
  beige page, cream panel, or the charcoal bands. **Seven colours failed and
  were corrected**, the worst at 2.01:1 against a 4.5:1 floor:

  | Used for | Was | Now | Ratio |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Eyebrow labels, accents | `#9A7B4F` | `#6A5030` | 2.50 -> 4.74 |
  | Tab strip, panel labels, plaque terms | `#8A7E6A` | `#5A5344` | 2.52 -> 4.82 |
  | Bronze text on the dark bands | `#B08A55` | `#C6A26B` | 3.50 -> 4.65 |
  | Muted text on the dark bands | `#A89F8F` | `#B4AB9B` | 4.25 -> 4.89 |
  | Secondary body text | `#6B6459` | `#5D574D` | 3.70 -> 4.52 |
  | Chart and caption bronze | `#8A6E45` | `#685334` | 3.28 -> 4.53 |
  | Muted band text | `#8A8172` | `#ABA499` | 2.79 -> 4.60 |

  Decorative bronze - rules, borders, hover states, gradient fills - is
  unchanged, because it carries no information on its own. Body text was
  already comfortable (5.93:1 on the page, 7.40:1 on panels). A regression test
  recomputes every ratio from the stylesheet, so a future palette edit that
  breaks contrast fails the build rather than shipping.
- **The Risk Topology map has a text alternative.** The map is drawn on canvas,
  which a screen reader cannot inspect. The five canvas layers are marked
  decorative and a live description carries the same numbers in sentences:
  starting beta and volatility, the tail loss, the value at risk, the share of
  paths touching the danger perimeter, the perimeter itself, and how many assets
  and pairings are plotted. It is generated from the same simulation the picture
  is drawn from, so the two cannot drift apart.
- **Keyboard:** interactive elements take focus and show a visible bronze focus
  ring; the page is operable without a mouse.
- **Motion:** every animation is wrapped in `prefers-reduced-motion`, and the
  map's particle loop idles when off-screen. Nothing flashes.
- **Structure:** one `<h1>`, section headings in order, tables with real header
  cells, and wide content scrolls inside its own container rather than forcing
  the page sideways.
- **Meaning is never carried by colour alone.** Risk verdicts, exclusions and
  data-quality states are always stated in words as well.

## Known gaps

These are real. They are listed because a statement that claims full conformance
would be an overclaim, and this project's whole premise is not overclaiming.

1. **Streamlit's own widgets.** Sliders, multiselects, tabs and expanders are
   generated by the Streamlit framework. Their markup, focus order and ARIA
   labelling are outside this project's control, and some of them fall short of
   AA. Meleona styles them but does not rewrite them.
2. **Charts.** Plotly figures expose limited keyboard and screen-reader support.
   Every chart is accompanied by the numbers in text — the headline verdict, the
   tables and the audit trail — but the interactive chart itself is not an
   equivalent experience.
3. **The map's interaction, not its content.** The text description covers what
   the map says. It does not reproduce hovering a unit or dragging the terrain.
4. **No user testing yet.** Contrast is computed and the markup was reviewed by
   hand, but this app has not been tested end to end by someone using a screen
   reader or switch access daily. That is the most valuable thing still missing,
   and no automated check substitutes for it.

## Reporting a barrier

Email **meleona.support@gmail.com** with the session reference from the page
footer and what you were trying to do. Barriers that block someone from using
the engine are treated as defects, not feature requests, and fixed ahead of
other work. If a fix is slow, you will get the underlying numbers by email in a
format that works for you.

## How this was assessed

Self-assessment on 2026-08-26: computed contrast ratios for every palette pair,
a manual review of headings, focus, motion and alternative text, and automated
regression tests for the contrast values and the map description. No third-party
audit has been carried out, and none is claimed.
